## TACTICS

### MECHANISM 1 — Identify Which Game You Are Playing (prerequisite to all other steps)
1. Determine your monetization model per channel
2. If monetizing via CPMs/AdSense/brand deals → you are playing the **Awareness Game** (entertainment creator)
3. If monetizing via back-end offers, products, services → you are playing the **Conversion Game** (education creator)
4. Rule: one game per channel only; can run both simultaneously across separate channels

---

### MECHANISM 2 — Winning the Awareness Game (4-attribute content checklist)
Run every content idea through all four filters before producing:

**A. Shareworthiness**
1. Ask: will the viewer believe they get social/professional credit by sharing this?
2. Credit transfers only if recipient has NOT seen it before → idea must be first or meaningfully different in the space
3. Target "super shares" from other entertainment creators (worth 1,000× a normal share) by making concept novel enough that curating it builds their audience's trust in them
4. Standard for super share is higher than for passive viewer share — novelty bar must be crossed

**B. Curiosity-Inducing Hook**
1. Identify the conventional belief the viewer already holds about your topic
2. Open the video by contrasting that belief against a proposed new reality
3. Format: "[Something] is going to change how [viewer identity] does [thing they believe they already know]"
4. This contrast creates an open loop the viewer cannot close without watching → extends average view duration

**C. Emotional Payoff at End**
1. Use words, tone, topic, visuals, music to evoke a named emotion by video end
2. Frame your point against what the viewer wants most (contrast again) to trigger shock, fear, or anger — highest-share emotions
3. No emotional response = no share incentive = no credit to earn

**D. Large Total Addressable Market (TAM)**
1. Pick ideas that are simple enough or broadly relatable enough for the largest possible audience in your category
2. Test: "Drilling to the center of the earth" vs. "organic chemistry" — choose the former
3. Test: "Kim Kardashian getting her law degree" vs. "Section 257 of the bar" — choose the former
4. Broader TAM → more user minutes → algorithm pushes harder

**Volume rule:** consistent execution of all four attributes at high volume wins; execution is secondary to idea selection

---

### MECHANISM 3 — Winning the Conversion Game (4-attribute content checklist)
Run every content idea through all four filters:

**A. On-Target Shareworthiness**
1. Same credit-transfer psychology as awareness game, but credit is specifically a "professional hat tip" among peers
2. Solve must be non-obvious AND previously unknown to the recipient — if they've seen it, credit doesn't transfer
3. Must be first or different on the topic within the niche
4. Target super shares from other education creators in space — they will share if content is non-obvious and tactical enough to build their credibility

**B. Large On-Target TAM**
1. Restrict topic selection strictly to subjects that are tactically valuable to potential buyers of your product
2. Do NOT broaden aperture to chase views — trains viewer brain that you don't always solve their problems
3. Discipline in topic selection builds the habit: "this creator solves my problems"

**C. Problem/Solution Clarity**
1. Frame problem and solution so clearly that misunderstanding is impossible
2. Use simple frames, simple words, high comprehension
3. Clarity is MORE critical here than in awareness game because niche depth requires it
4. Reference: speaker's other videos on hooks and script writing for word-level precision

**D. Tactically Implementable, Non-Obvious Solve**
1. Binary test: "Could the viewer walk away and make a change that improves their situation?"
2. If yes → educational content, satisfies condition
3. If no → it is entertainment in disguise, does not satisfy condition
4. "Walkway value" is the exact term used

**Volume rule:** same as awareness — most volume wins

---

### MECHANISM 4 — Idea Sourcing (applies to both games)
1. Study existing content in your category
2. Identify outliers — videos that dramatically over-performed (either from deliberate strategy or accidental insight)
3. Reverse-engineer why they worked against the relevant 4-attribute checklist
4. Remix: take the proven premise, produce your own version
5. For education creators: also monitor entertainment creators in your space — they often find strong premises but execute poorly; extract the premise and deliver it tactically
6. Tool pitched for steps 1–4: Sandcastles.ai

---

### MECHANISM 5 — Algorithm Optimization (universal, both games)
Three proxy metrics the algorithm uses to measure session-extension ability:
1. **Average View Duration (AVD)** — how long did viewer watch
2. **Engagement rate** — likes, comments
3. **Platform bounce rate** — % who watched then left the platform entirely

Content must optimize for: **high shares + high AVD**
- High AVD → elongates existing user sessions → more user minutes → algorithm pushes more
- Shares → new sessions → more user minutes → algorithm pushes more
