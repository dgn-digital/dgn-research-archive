## TACTICS (MECHANISMS)

### 1. Pre-Job Plumbing Planning Sequence for Luxury Builds
1. Start with basement — establish sewer elevation and available pitch before anything else
2. Identify ledge/soil constraints early; calculate math on pitch limitations
3. Determine water entry point collaboratively (builder + plumber) — prioritize short run to meter to prevent unauthorized tap risk and minimize under-slab piping
4. Confirm fixture locations and wet wall layout before rough-in
5. Establish quality standard explicitly in contract/conversation — "artistic" neatness is not assumed; must be communicated
6. Keep builder/decision-maker on-site for real-time approvals — avoids rework when dealing with architects, designers, and multiple subs

### 2. Dual-Meter Setup for Irrigation/Outdoor Water (Cost Saving)
1. Run municipal water through constant-pressure pump (Little Giant) to address low street pressure
2. Split into two meters post-pump: Meter 1 = interior domestic water (charged for water in + sewer out); Meter 2 = irrigation/hose bibs (charged for water in only, no sewer charge)
3. Result: municipality only charges sewer on water that enters and exits the home; outdoor water bypasses sewer billing

### 3. Constant Pressure Pump + Expansion Tank Safety System
1. Install constant pressure pump (Little Giant) when street pressure is inadequate
2. Install check valve downstream of pump — prevents back-expansion into main
3. Install expansion tank with diaphragm — absorbs excess pressure from thermal expansion of hot water
4. Install pressure relief valve as final failsafe — releases water if pump over-pressurizes system
5. Without expansion tank on a closed system: risk of pipe/fitting failure from over-pressurization

### 4. Recirculating Hot Water Loop Layout
1. Identify furthest fixture from water heater
2. Run hot water supply loop to that fixture and return — keeps main line at temperature continuously
3. For very large homes: install multiple return lines depending on layout
4. Design fixture placement during framing phase to minimize pipe run length — reduces wait time and water waste
5. Pair with heat pump water heater; note: heat pump unit produces less peak volume than gas — set client expectations accordingly

### 5. Island Sink Venting (Bow Vent / Island Vent)
1. Obtain plumbing inspector approval for bow vent configuration before rough-in
2. Run drain from sink; at maximum achievable height within island wall cavity, execute U-turn — drain and vent share same pipe above flood rim level (approximately 6 inches above sink basin rim)
3. Line then drops down; vent portion ties back to a vertical stack elsewhere in the structure
4. Install cleanout on vent side
5. Configure vent leg to drain back toward fixture — prevents water from sitting in vent line if backflow occurs

### 6. Exterior Sink / Outdoor Kitchen Winterization Sequence
1. Install outdoor sink on dedicated isolated line (not tied to recirculating loop) with interior isolation valve
2. At season end: close isolation valve, drain all lines
3. Add antifreeze to P-trap beneath outdoor sink
4. Treat same as irrigation winterization — can be performed by plumber on service plan or competent homeowner
5. Specify frost-free hose bibs with 12-inch stem (water shutoff 12 inches inside building envelope); stainless preferred over copper/brass for freeze resistance

### 7. Aqua Hose Bib Installation Notes
- Flush-mount Quick Connect system
- Stem shuts off water 12 inches into house (inside heated envelope)
- When Quick Connect is removed, residual water drains from fixture for ~1–2 seconds (confirms stem shutoff is inside envelope)
- Still recommended: shut off interior valve and drain before winter even with frost-free units

### 8. Second-Floor Laundry Flood Mitigation System
1. Install Killarney zero-entry stainless steel appliance pan (1-inch lip, trough drain at front) — set flush with subfloor so machines slide in without lifting; hardwood floor tucks in behind pan edge
2. Pan handles small/slow leaks and condensate; explicitly not sized for catastrophic washer hose failure
3. Run 1-inch drain from pan by gravity to basement mechanical room
4. Terminate in **indirect drain** (air gap — not direct connection to drain stack) — prevents backflow if downstream stoppage occurs
5. Connect condensate lines from furnaces, air handlers, and water heater to same indirect drain
6. To prevent indirect trap from drying out: install **trap primer** tied to nearby fixture (toilet flush or faucet use delivers slug of water to trap automatically)
7. Catastrophic failure handling: *transcript cuts off mid-answer* — method not captured

### 9. Wall-Hung Toilet Installation Requirements
1. Determine finish height before tile — adjustment after tile is prohibitively expensive
2. Standard target: "comfort height" = former handicap height (~17–18 inches); adjust per

**Thermostat strategy for geothermal:**

**Superheater (desuperheater) hot water integration:**
1. Waste heat captured from geothermal refrigerant cycle
2. Routed to preheat holding/buffer tank
3. Preheated water feeds standard water heater → water heater works less
4. Net effect: near-free hot water from otherwise-wasted heat
