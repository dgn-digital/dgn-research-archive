# STRATEGIC RESEARCH SYNTHESIS

**Source characterisation up front:** 24 of these 25 videos appear to be from a single creator ("Frankie Finn") teaching agency-owner client acquisition, or from closely aligned creators sharing the same playbook. This is a near-monoculture, not a diverse market survey. Treat the "patterns" below as *one guru's method repeated across clips*, not an independently verified industry consensus. I cannot verify Frankie Finn's claimed track record (20 years, 3 agencies, 100+/200+ clients, "best-selling books") — none of it is evidenced on-screen and the client-count figure drifts (100+ in Videos 7/15, 200+ in Video 11).

## 1. PATTERNS — what shows up repeatedly

**A. Sell the outcome, not the deliverable.** The single most repeated idea across the batch. "A deliverable with a price tag is never an offer" (Videos 3, 8, 22). Reframe the *service* (GHL, SEO, Facebook ads, websites) as the *result the client actually wants* (flood job calls, MVA cases, mommy-makeover patients, new-construction jobs). Evidence: Videos 8, 10, 13, 14, 16, 20, 23; explicit price contrast in 8uFNx2Q_Ag4 ($500–1,000 pushback → $10,000 no pushback).

**B. Niche by *person-type/situation*, not service-type.** "Category ≠ niche." The niche is the client's most expensive job (Video 15). Roofers → commercial flat roofing (£400k jobs vs £99 repairs). Plumbers → new construction (Videos 20, 24). Plastic surgeons → mommy makeovers (Videos 9, 10, 14, 15). Restoration → residential category-3 flood (Videos 6, 13). 48-hour rule for picking a category (Video 15).

**C. Lead with a "give, not a take."** Cold outreach should open with a free idea or a visual mockup, never "jump on a call" (Videos 18, 19, 23, 24, 25). Make it about *them* — count "I/we" vs "you/your" (Videos 11, 23, 24). Keep message 1 to 3–4 sentences; its only job is to start a conversation (Video 23).

**D. Demonstrate, don't describe.** "Say less, demonstrate more" (Video 11). Send a pre-built sample landing page/funnel mockup with the prospect's branding (Videos 17, 18, 23, 24). Loom/video closing replaces sales calls (Videos 4, 5, 7, 11).

**E. Target proven buyers, not "barefoot prospects."** Dan Kennedy "200 pairs of shoes" framework, explicitly credited (Video 11). Buyer signals: running paid ads / pixel on site / masterminds / CRMs / hiring / trade suppliers (Videos 11, 22). Two sales required: (1) should they spend at all, (2) with you (Video 11).

**F. Do market research inside communities first.** Join Facebook/LinkedIn/Reddit/Quora groups, extract repeated pain points, identify the jobs clients *don't* want and invert (Videos 4, 6, 20, 21). A recurring "market research bot" is offered free as a lead magnet (Videos 4, 15, 21).

**G. Recurring / passive positioning.** Salespeople and websites close deals so the founder is removed from sales; recurring billing (Videos 1, 5). "Beach, less-laptop lifestyle" is the aspirational hook (Videos 15, 23).
