## 6. CONTRADICTIONS & CAUTIONS

**Internal contradictions:**
- **Revenue qualifier drifts** even within one video — Video 10 says "$1M/yr" then "$2M/yr" minutes apart. The gate is a sales device, not a real filter.
- **The "$2–4K agencies are useless" vs. own hidden price.** Video 21 says real SEO costs $10–20K/month while Videos 15/19/22 trash agencies charging $2–4K. If Igler charges in the $2–4K band he's criticising his own tier; if he charges $10K+ he's hiding a premium price behind "anti-hype." Either way the messaging is internally strained.
- **"Review velocity is a ranking signal" vs. "review response is *not* a ranking factor, just trust"** (Video 23) — mostly reconciled, but shows the ranking-factor claims are asserted, not evidenced.

**Claims that look like hype, not evidence:**
- **"$20M generated for clients," "over 100 businesses," "$571K in 9 months," "$1.27M in 90 days," "6.9x ROAS"** (Videos 4, 8, 9, 10, 16) — all self-reported, ScreenTitan screenshots at best, no third-party verification, clients unnamed. The Video 10 anti-hype line ("this is just their real numbers") is itself a persuasion device wrapped around unverifiable figures. Treat as marketing.
- **Precise-sounding stats with no source:** "99.2% of home service websites under 1% conversion" (Video 7), "75%+ of local searches click top-3" (Video 22, "multiple studies" unnamed), "300% review increase" (Video 15), "70–80% of leads from map pack" (Video 19). Directionally plausible, but the false precision is a red flag.
- **"Keyword-difficulty 8.69 → 1.53"** (Videos 3, 10) — cited as proof but difficulty-score movement isn't revenue; it's a vanity metric dressed as outcome.

**Things I cannot verify (stated plainly):**
- **"Local Dominator," "Contractor Commerce," "Natles HVAC," "Nexus Water Damage Restoration Orlando"** — I cannot confirm these tools/companies exist or perform as claimed. I'm not asserting they're fabricated; I simply can't verify them.
- **Tommy Mello / A1 Garage Door** is a genuinely documented operator, but the specific "$6.8M client result" and "$1B valuation" figures (Video 1) are self-reported within promotional content.
- **The name-match / DBA tactic's current effectiveness** — Google's local spam policies change frequently; I can't verify these tactics still work as described or comply with current guidelines. Videos 6 and 12 themselves flag active crackdowns.

**Strategic caution for you specifically:** The most-repeated tactics in this entire library (exact-match DBA naming, multi-GBP radius gaming) sit in a **grey-hat zone** that conflicts with a "trust-first, premium, anti-hype" brand. Adopting the *positioning language* here would put you in a crowded, samey lane. Your edge is the opposite: real trade ownership, UK specificity, transparent pricing, and AI tools as *product* rather than as ranking trick.
