## TACTICS
*(Note: This is a journalistic/explainer video, not instructional. No repeatable step-by-step mechanisms are taught. The following are the closest equivalents — technical processes described in sequence.)*

**How grid inertia stabilizes frequency during a supply loss:**
1. Large spinning generators (coal, gas, nuclear, hydro) maintain rotational mass at constant speed during normal operation
2. A power plant suddenly goes offline → supply/demand imbalance occurs
3. The spinning device detects the imbalance on the grid
4. It draws on stored rotational inertia, injecting power to compensate
5. It loses a small amount of rotational speed in doing so, converting that kinetic energy into electricity
6. Grid frequency is stabilized without blackout

**How solar displaces inertia (mechanism behind the Spain blackout):**
1. Solar panels convert sunlight directly to electricity — no spinning generator, no inertia
2. Spain tripled solar capacity over five years
3. Several solar plants suddenly disconnected
4. Insufficient inertia existed to restore supply/demand balance
5. Domino disconnection cascade → full grid collapse

**Synchronous compensator as inertia substitute:**
1. Device is identical mechanically to a spinning generator in a conventional plant
2. Remove the fuel source (coal/gas) — retain only the spinning mass
3. Deploy on grid alongside renewable generation
4. Device spins continuously (example given: 100-ton unit at 1,500 rpm)
5. Provides inertia buffer equivalent to fossil-fuel plant without burning fuel
6. Enables grid to absorb renewable variability without blackout risk

**Veir superconducting cable production (current state, 2025):**
1. Superconducting material fabricated into cable sections
