# AI Thumbnails Research Synthesis
## For DGN Digital (with DGN Gas as pilot use-case)

**Upfront caveat:** These 3 videos are tutorial/creator content, not business-strategy sources. None address B2B video marketing for trades, and none provide CTR data. Treat the "viral" framing in your query as unsupported by this evidence — nobody here proves virality, they show *process*. I've flagged unverifiable tools throughout.

---

## 1. PATTERNS (what shows up repeatedly)

- **AI generates a starting point, humans/tools finish it.** All three treat AI as one stage in a chain, not a one-click solution. MrBeast uses AI for *mockups* handed to artists (V1). Jason routes ChatGPT → Nano Banana → Photoshop (V2). Himanshu runs AI image → face swap → Canva → Lightroom (V3).
- **Face/personal presence matters.** V2 and V3 both insist on inserting a real personal photo (upload in Nano Banana; Discord face-swap in V3). This is a strong signal for you: a *face* on the thumbnail beats generic AI art.
- **Multi-tool workflows, not single tools.** Nobody uses "just AI." Every credible-looking process stitches 3–5 tools together.
- **"AI vs human artist" defensiveness** appears in V1 and V2 — both pre-empt the accusation that AI replaces designers. Signals the audience is sensitive to this.
- **Cost-saving as the headline benefit** (V2 explicitly: replaces $500/mo designer).

## 2. OUTLIERS

- **V3 (Himanshu) is the only one with actual craft substance** — rule of thirds, colour psychology, specific Lightroom preset values, "2–3 words max." Whether it *works* is unproven (no CTR data), but it's the only source that treats thumbnails as a discipline rather than a novelty. **This is the model to emulate.**
- **V1 (MrBeast) is an outlier on scale** — "hundreds of thousands per month" and a large payroll team. Irrelevant to your economics; useful only as a rhetorical proof point ("even MrBeast keeps humans in the loop").
- **V2 is the outlier on rigour** — makes the boldest claim ("AI looks better than my $500/mo designer") with the least evidence.

## 3. PRICING INTELLIGENCE

The "price ladder" here is **tool cost**, not service pricing — an important distinction for your business.

| Item | Cost | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Human thumbnail designer (small creator) | $500/month | V2 |
| MrBeast-scale thumbnail production | "hundreds of thousands/month" | V1 |
| Nano Banana paid (watermark removal) | ~$30/month | V2 *(cannot verify this tool/pricing)* |
| ChatGPT | Free tier claimed sufficient | V2 |
| Canva / Lightroom | Freemium | V3 |

**The commercial insight for DGN Digital:** the market anchor for "done-for-you thumbnails" sits around **$500/month for a human** (V2). AI collapses the *cost of production* to near-zero — which means the value you sell is **not the image, it's the system, the strategy, and the time saved.** Don't compete on "cheap thumbnails"; that race goes to zero.

## 4. GAPS & OPPORTUNITIES (what nobody covers — angles you could own)

1. **Nobody shows CTR / performance data.** All three claim results; none prove them. A trades-focused service that *reports actual view/click data* would immediately out-credential these creators. This aligns with your trust-first, anti-hype positioning.
2. **Nobody addresses B2B / local-service video.** All content is creator-economy YouTube. Thumbnails for a *heating engineer's* case-study video, before/after boiler installs, or trust-building explainers is completely uncovered.
3. **Nobody discusses brand consistency / templated systems** — creators optimise per-video for chaos-clicks. A trade business needs *consistent, professional* thumbnails that build recognition. Opposite goal.
4. **"Legal clickbaiting" (V3) is a liability, not an asset for trades.** Your buyers (luxury homeowners) are trust-sensitive. Sensationalism erodes the premium positioning. Gap = *credibility-first thumbnails*.
5. **Reusable presets/prompts as a productised asset** — V3's saved Lightroom preset and description-linked prompt hint at packaging. You could productise a "trades thumbnail system" (prompt library + preset + template).

## 5. RECOMMENDATIONS

**[TEST] Build a repeatable "trades thumbnail" workflow from V3's spine.**
Adopt Himanshu's chain (title-first → AI image → real face composited in → Canva text → colour-grade preset). It's the only method here with actual design logic. Test it on 5 DGN Gas videos and *measure CTR in YouTube Studio* — do the thing none of the videos did.

**[ADOPT NOW] Sell the system, not the image.**
Given AI drops production cost to ~$30/mo of tools (V2), do NOT position DGN Digital thumbnails as a cheap deliverable. Bundle them into your recurring care plan as "video assets that build trust and get found" — value-priced, not per-image. This protects premium positioning.

**[ADOPT NOW] Make "credibility-first, no clickbait" your explicit differentiator.**
Every source flirts with sensationalism (V3's "legal clickbaiting," V2's untested claims). For luxury-homeowner trades, that's poison. Own the opposite: professional thumbnails that a £15k boiler client would trust. This is a genuine market gap.

**[TEST] A/B split testing as a client-reportable feature.**
V2's 3-variant YouTube Studio split test is genuinely useful and cheap. Test it, then turn the *reporting* into a monthly client touchpoint ("here's what your best thumbnail earned"). Turns a tactic into recurring-plan value.

**[WATCH] Nano Banana + Discord face-swap tools.**
V2's "Nano Banana" and V3's "InsideFace Bot" — **I cannot verify these tools, their pricing, or reliability.** Face-swap bots also carry consent/likeness risk if used on real customers. Watch, verify independently, and check T&Cs before any client-facing use.

## 6. CONTRADICTIONS & CAUTIONS

- **AI as replacement vs. inspiration.** V1 explicitly says AI is *mockup only*, humans finish. V2 says AI *replaces* the human entirely and looks *better*. Direct contradiction. V1's stance (human-in-the-loop) is more defensible and, notably, comes from the highest-budget operator.
- **"Free tools just as good as paid" (V2)** — asserted, then immediately undercut by the speaker using paid ChatGPT Pro, paid Nano Banana, and paid Photoshop. Self-promotional overreach; discount it.
- **All "results" claims are unevidenced.** V2's "looks better than $500 designer" and V3's "psychology" framing are assertions with **zero CTR/analytics data** across all three videos. The "go viral" goal in your query is not supported by anything here.
- **V3's "psychology" is uncited.** The rule-of-thirds and colour claims are plausible design conventions but presented as fact without sources. Useful as hypotheses to test, not gospel.
- **Scale mismatch.** MrBeast's economics (V1) are irrelevant to your model — don't let "even MrBeast uses AI" become hype cover for over-claiming.
- **Likeness/consent risk:** face-swap workflows (V3) applied to real customers or staff need explicit permission — relevant given DGN Gas's premium, trust-based clientele.

**Bottom line:** This corpus gives you a *workflow* worth testing (V3) and a *pricing lesson* (don't sell images, sell trust-building systems). It gives you almost nothing on "going viral" — and for a premium trades brand, "viral" is probably the wrong goal anyway. Own **trustworthy and findable**, not viral.

---

## TACTICS
**Psychology/Layout:**
- Rule of Thirds: place main subject at grid intersections; left side = viewer perspective, right side = creator perspective
- Symmetrical Rule: used by only ~9% of creators
- Left-side placement prioritized — matches natural left-to-right reading habit
- 2–3 words max on thumbnail text (unless large established audience)
- Thumbnails can carry zero text if visual story is complete
- Colors that "pop" in post: yellow, red, orange-adjacent saturated tones — avoid black as hero color

**Workflow (sequential):**
1. **Imagine** → write title first, build thumbnail concept around it
2. **AI Image Generation** — prompt structure: *"Make a cinematic, realistic portrait image of a [subject], --ar 16:9"*
3. **Face Swap** — Discord bot "InsideFace Bot": `/save id` (upload your photo) → `/swap id` (upload AI image + saved name)
4. **Upscale** — Canva → Effects/Apps → Image Upscaler → 2x/4x/8x options
5. **Background removal + text/gradient** — done in Canva
6. **Color Grading** — Adobe Lightroom (iOS/Android):
   - Exposure: +0.44
   - Contrast: +15
   - Highlights: +2
   - Shadows: -8
   - Vibrance: +28 to +35 (uses 33)
   - Saturation: +5 to +12 (uses 10)
   - Vignette: -48
   - Clarity: +28 to +38 (uses 32)
   - Texture: +5 to +15 (uses 8)
   - Color Noise: +5 to +15 (uses 8)
   - Save as **preset** for reuse

---

## PROOF
- Claims 4 years of YouTube thumbnail-making
- Shows visual progression: first video thumbnail → 100th → current style
- States channel ran "5 types of thumbnail experiments" in the past year
- Mentions earning claim example: "10 lakhs per month" — explicitly clarifies **he did not earn it**, a guest/subject did; used as thumbnail text with disclosure in-video

---

- No CTR or analytics figures shared for any thumbnail style
- Prompt in description not read aloud in full — viewer must pause or visit externally
- "Psychology" framing is asserted, not sourced or cited
